How it works

  1. Add a classFeltReady gives it a join code and a link to send your students.
  2. Students joinThey sign in free (no password), open the link, and join under the name you know them by.
  3. They dealHands play out on their own. Every answer is graded and every mistake is explained, on a phone or a computer.
  4. You see the resultsEach student’s accuracy and speed by drill, and their most common mistakes.

What your students practice

  • Pot calls. Pot raises and re-pots, six- or nine-handed, at blinds from $1/$2 to $25/$50. True pot or rounded pot, to match your room.
  • Side pots. Build each side pot at the end of the betting round when someone is all in for less, including two or more all-ins and dead money.
  • Showdown reads. Pick the two hole cards and three board cards that play, side pots first, with chops and odd chips.
  • Double board bomb pots. Split each pot between the boards and read both boards, quarters included.
  • An optional timer from relaxed to fast, and a daily challenge: the same five hands for every dealer, with a leaderboard.

What you see as an instructor

  • Every class at a glance: students, how many practiced this week, answers this week, and overall accuracy.
  • Each student’s accuracy and average time per drill, and the mistakes they make most (like re-pots, side pots with two all-ins, or chops).
  • Sort students by lowest accuracy to see who needs help, or by last active to see who isn’t practicing.
  • Close a class to new students, make a new code, or remove a student.

Students’ privacy is built in: you see the name they joined under and their answers from the day they joined. You don’t see their email, anything from before they joined, or anything after they leave. More on the privacy page.
