Privacy

Last updated November 17, 2026

FeltReady (feltready.com) is a poker dealer trainer run by Stephen, in Texas. This page says what FeltReady keeps about you, who sees it, and how to have it deleted. The short version: it keeps what it needs to run the trainer, it doesn’t sell anything about you, and it has no ads.

If you practice without an account

Your hands, answers, and settings stay in your browser. To count visitors, FeltReady gives your browser a random id, kept in its local storage, and records:

  • where you came from (the site that linked you, or a tag in the link, like utm_source) and the first page you opened
  • how many hands you played and questions you answered each day, and whether you opened the sign-in box or asked for a code
  • which account that browser signs in to, if it ever does

FeltReady doesn’t set cookies for this or store your IP address with it, and the random id doesn’t say who you are.

If you sign in

  • Your email address, to sign you in with an emailed code. If you add a passkey, your device keeps the private key; FeltReady only stores the public part it needs to check you.
  • Your settings and stats, so they follow you to other devices.
  • Every answer you give: which drill and game, details of the spot (like the pot size), your answer, whether it was right, how long it took, and the kind of mistake. This is what your weak spots page is built from.
  • How many hands you play each day, in each game.
  • The daily challenge: your answers, score, and time, and the leaderboard name you pick. Your leaderboard name and score are public on that day’s leaderboard. Your email never is.

If you join a class

When you join a class with your instructor’s code, that school or card room’s owners and instructors can see:

  • the name you gave when you joined
  • your answers and times from the moment you joined: how many, how many were right, by drill, and your most common mistakes

They don’t see your email address, and they never see answers from before you joined or after you leave. You can leave a class any time in your account settings. Your instructor can also remove you. Rejoining starts fresh from the day you rejoin.

If you send feedback or ask about a pilot

FeltReady keeps your message and, if you give one, your email address, so it can write back. Feedback also includes which page and game you were on, your screen width, and your browser type, which helps track down bugs. A pilot request from the For schools page also keeps your name and your school’s name.

What FeltReady does with it

It uses this information to run the trainer, show you your stats, rank the daily challenge, show instructors how their students are doing, answer messages, and decide what to build next. It doesn’t sell or rent your information, use it for ads, or share it with anyone except the services below and the instructors of classes you join.

Services FeltReady uses

  • Supabase hosts the database and handles sign-in, including the emails with your sign-in codes.
  • Cloudflare hosts the site.
  • Google Fonts provides the site’s fonts.

Like any web host, these services see your IP address and basic browser details when your browser loads pages or talks to the database, and may keep them in their own logs. Their own privacy policies cover that.

The DownTracker links on FeltReady add a tag saying you came from FeltReady. Nothing about you goes with it. DownTracker is a separate app with its own privacy policy.

How long it’s kept, and deleting it

Your account information is kept while you have an account. To delete your account and everything tied to it (settings, stats, answers, daily challenge entries, and class memberships), sign in and use Send feedback at the bottom of any game page to ask for it. Sending it while signed in shows the request came from you. You can also ask for a copy of your information the same way.

Feedback you sent is kept after your account is deleted, but no longer tied to the account. To clear your signed-out practice from a browser, clear this site’s data in your browser settings.

Age

FeltReady is meant for adults training to deal or working as dealers. It isn’t meant for anyone under 18, and it doesn’t knowingly keep information about children.

Security

Data goes over encrypted connections. In the database, each member can only reach their own rows, and schools only reach what this page describes. No system is perfectly secure, though, so FeltReady can’t promise nothing will ever go wrong.

Changes

If this page changes, the date at the top changes too. If a change affects what instructors can see, or adds a new use of your information, FeltReady will say so on the site before it takes effect.

Questions

Use Send feedback at the bottom of any game page. Schools can also use the form on the For schools page.
